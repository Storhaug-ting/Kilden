# Support

## Asking a question

Open an [issue](https://github.com/Storhaug-ting/Kilden/issues). That is the only
channel, and it is deliberate: a question and its answer stay readable for the
next person who asks it.

Search the existing issues first.

## What this repository can answer

- What a source is, who published it, and when it was retrieved.
- Whether a source still matches the original.
- How the conversion works and why the markdown looks the way it does.
- How to add a source, which is documented in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What it cannot

**Nothing here is legal advice, and nothing here is binding.** The sources are
reproductions of documents other people published, kept so they can be cited and
compared against the original. A question about what a law or a guide *means* for
a specific case belongs with a lawyer, or with the publisher.

A question about a specific project belongs in that project's own repository.
This one holds external material only.

## Reporting something wrong

- A conversion that misrepresents the original, a dead link, or a source whose
  checksum no longer matches — open an issue.
- Anything that lets a source be tampered with undetected — see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report it privately.
